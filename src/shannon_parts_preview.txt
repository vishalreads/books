Microphones can be omnidirectional, designed
to pick up sound from all directions, or unidirec-
tional, designed to pick up sound from one direc-
tion only. However, even unidirectional ones pick
up some sound from all directions.
To make a microphone highly directional, it is
necessary to place it inside something that nar-
rows or concentrates sound. The first of these is
the parabolic reflector, which is much the same
as a TV satellite dish on a smaller scale. It can be
as small as a few inches across, such as the one
made by Tyco as part of its line of Spy-Tech toys
(which really works) to a tylical hand-held plas-
tic L8-inch model, which works much better than
the toy one, to the four-foot tripod-mounted mod-
el from CSI.
