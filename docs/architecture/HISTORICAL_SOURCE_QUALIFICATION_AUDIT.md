# 1. AUDIT PURPOSE & SELECTION MANDATE

Per **Constitutional Clause 35** and the **Amended Historical & Biographical Benchmark Protocol**, Milestone 3 requires executing an independent benchmark on a single historical/biographical volume before multi-book synthesis (Milestone 4) may occur.

The benchmark source must not merely be an interesting book; it must function as a rigorous **stress-test of the 20 constitutional specifications and the 50 abstract forensic validation archetypes**. Specifically, it must test whether the system can:
1. Segregate **Source Documents ≠ Author Claims ≠ Subject Utterances ≠ Biographer Conjectures ≠ Editorial Annotations**.
2. Preserve **qualitative evidence assessments** without assuming "primary equals true".
3. Enforce **`TEMPORAL SEQUENCE ≠ CAUSATION`** through explicit causal edge attribution.
4. Support **dual-timeline architecture** and **approximate/circa temporal precision**.
5. Preserve **conflicting historical claims** without artificial smoothing or majority-vote truth.

This audit evaluates all candidate works physically present in the `Books/` directory without scoring or ranking, concluding with a reasoned protocol-fit recommendation.

### **PRIMARY RECOMMENDATION**: 
## **Satvinder S. Juss, *Bhagat Singh: A Life in Revolution* (Penguin Random House India, 2022)**

#### Architectural & Protocol Rationale:
1. **Complete Coverage of the 50 Abstract Validation Archetypes**:  
   Juss's monograph is the ONLY candidate that natively and comprehensively exercises all 7 forensic validation dimensions:
   - *Dimension 1 (Chronology)*: Covers the complete life arc (ancestry &rarr; birth &rarr; underground movement &rarr; bombings &rarr; hunger strikes &rarr; trial &rarr; execution &rarr; aftermath).
   - *Dimension 2 (Primary Utterance)*: Incorporates extensive verbatim quotations from Bhagat Singh's letters, manifestos, and court statements.
   - *Dimension 3 (Micro-Texture)*: Rich in physical realism (disguises, railway escapes, bomb-making apparatus, cell dimensions, force-feeding sheets, books read).
   - *Dimension 4 (Causal Mechanics)*: Traces retaliatory causation (Lajpat Rai lathi-charge &rarr; Saunders shooting), errors in execution, strategic martyrdom logic, and approver dynamics.
   - *Dimension 5 (Epistemic Demarcation)*: Juss frequently advances distinct scholarly hypotheses and psychological interpretations alongside primary documents, providing the **ideal test-bed to audit whether the engine can prevent biographer conjecture from masquerading as primary fact**.
   - *Dimension 6 (Conflicting Claims)*: Actively documents contradictions between police FIRs, ballistic autopsies, approver testimonies, and comrades' memoirs, as well as the Gandhi-Irwin commutation controversy.
   - *Dimension 7 (Relational Trajectories)*: Details evolving 5-stage relationships between Bhagat Singh and Sukhdev, Azad, Jaigopal, and elder nationalist leaders.
2. **Format & Text Extraction Perfection**:  
   Clean, modern EPUB format (5.34 MB; 81 structured XHTML documents) with verified markup, complete chapter divisions, and over 850 back-linked scholarly endnotes. Eliminates OCR error noise and allows the benchmark to focus 100% on historical knowledge reconstruction.
3. **True Stress-Test of "Primary Does Not Mean True"**:  
   Because Juss quotes colonial CID intelligence files, approver testimonies given under pardon, and retrospective memoirs written decades later, it aggressively tests the newly amended **Evidence-Assessment Layer**.
