The Evolutionary Astrology of Midpoint Composite Charts & The Eternal Triangle

Authors: Steven Forrest & Jodie Forrest
School: Evolutionary Relationship Synastry
Core Technique: Midpoint Composite Chart
Standard: Replacement-Grade Technical Master
"When two people commit themselves to loving each other, the whole of that couple is something new, something distinct from either of the individuals... Every couple, then, is a threesome: you, me, and what we are together. That ghostly but powerful presence is symbolized by the composite chart."

The Epistemological Paradigm: The Third Entity

Steven and Jodie Forrest establish the definitive masterclass on Composite Charts. Rather than treating relationship astrology merely as interpersonal cross-aspects (Synastry), they demonstrate that every relationship produces an emergent, autonomous meta-personality—a Third Entity with its own natal chart, soul mission, karmic past, and evolutionary trajectory.

Tier Level Astrological Tool Diagnostic Function
Tier 1: Base Individual Natal Charts Examines each person's innate psychological capacity for intimacy, personal wounds, and blind spots.
Tier 2: Interpersonal Bi-Wheel Synastry Measures chemistry, friction, emotional trigger points, and day-to-day interpersonal communication.
Tier 3: Transpersonal The Composite Chart Reveals the shared identity, karmic contracts, spiritual purpose, and autonomous destiny of the union.
UNIT 01

The Three-Tier Synastry Pyramid: Individual, Cross-Aspects, and the Third Entity

Chapters 1 & 3: Nuts and Bolts & The Eternal Triangle

Every couple is an Eternal Triangle composed of Partner A, Partner B, and the Relationship itself. The Composite Chart is calculated via mathematical midpoints of all planetary pairs and house cusps.

The Composite Chart serves as an arbiter, tie-breaker, and context generator. It explains why individuals behave differently together than they do when alone.

UNIT 02

Chapter 4: The Lunar Nodes
  • Composite South Node: The repository of shared past-life history, unresolved agreements, and familiar emotional ruts where the couple risks stagnating.
  • Dispositor of South Node: Pinpoints the exact karmic bottleneck and historical trauma of the couple.

UNIT 03

The Solar Purpose and Lunar Matrix: Composite Sun and Moon Dynamics

Chapters 5 & 6: Composite Sun and Moon

The Composite Sun is the vital heart and external mission of the union—what the couple came here to accomplish in the world. The Composite Moon is the private domestic climate: how the couple feels when the front door closes and they sit in silence together.

UNIT 04

The Public Face and Entry Gate: The Composite Ascendant and Angular Axis

Chapter 7: The Composite Ascendant

The Composite Ascendant describes the mask of the relationship—how society perceives the couple, and the environmental atmosphere of their initial meeting. The Midheaven (MC) governs their public legacy and career status, while the IC anchors their domestic and ancestral foundation.

UNIT 09

The Political Typology of the Third Entity: Power Balances in the Triangle

Chapter 3: The Eternal Triangle — Clinical Typologies

The Forrests map three primary relational political balances:

  • Culture Shock: The Composite is alien to both natal charts, pulling both partners into unexpected adventures.
  • Home Field Advantage: The Composite mirrors one partner's chart, giving them natural dominance while requiring extra effort to include the other.
  • Mutual Empowerment: The Composite activates the North Nodes of both individuals, catalyzing mutual evolution.
UNIT 10

Dynamic Forecasting and Timing: Transits and Progressions to the Composite Chart

Part Three: Transits, Progressions & Relationship Lifecycles

The Composite Chart is a living organism with its own developmental cycles. Key timing milestones:

  • Saturn conjunct Composite Angles: Classic indicator of formal marriage vows, or legal divorce if the structure is hollow.
  • Uranus to Composite Luminaries: Radical changes in lifestyle, relocations, or demands for independence.
  • Pluto to Composite Core: Deep psychological purging, death-and-rebirth transformation, and soul-level renewal.
