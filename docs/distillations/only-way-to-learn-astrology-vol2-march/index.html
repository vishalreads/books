The Seven Marc Edmund Jones Chart Shapes

Before analyzing signs or aspects, the master astrologer audits how planets are distributed across the 360-degree circle:

1. The Splash Pattern

Geometry: Planets evenly dispersed around the entire wheel ($360^circ$).

Archetype: The Universal Renaissance Mind. Broad interests, versatile, wide perspective; risks lack of concentration.

2. The Bundle Pattern

Geometry: All ten planets clustered within an arc of $120^circ$ or less.

Archetype: The Hyper-Focused Specialist. Unwavering single-pointed drive; risks extreme narrowness in life scope.

3. The Bowl Pattern

Geometry: All planets occupy one exact hemisphere ($180^circ$).

Archetype: The Cause-Driven Voyager. The Leading Planet acts as the spearhead; constantly pulls the empty hemisphere inward.

4. The Bucket (Funnel)

Geometry: Nine planets in a bowl, with ONE singleton handle planet opposite.

Archetype: The Single-Pointed Crusader. The handle planet absorbs and focalizes the entire chart's psychic energy.

5. The Locomotive

Geometry: Occupies $240^circ$ with an open $120^circ$ gap.

Archetype: The Self-Propelled Dynamic Engine. The leading clockwise planet acts as the locomotive engine driving the life.

6. The See-Saw

Geometry: Two opposing clusters separated by two large empty gaps ($60^circ+$).

Archetype: The Dialectical Arbitrator. Sees both sides of every issue; balances polarities, relationships, and diplomacy.

7. The Splay

Geometry: Three or more irregular clusters/stelliums around the wheel.

Archetype: The Rugged Individualist. Unconventional, maverick talents; refuses to fit into societal templates.
