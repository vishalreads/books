Axiom: Soul memory transcends physical birth; early karma carries past-life momentum.

Forensic Content: Mukunda retains clear, vivid conscious recollections of past Himalayan incarnations. During infancy, his mother brings him before her revered guru, Lahiri Mahasaya. The great master places the infant on his lap, touches his spiritual eye (Ajna center), and prophesies: “Little mother, thy son will be a yogi. As a spiritual engine, he will carry many souls to God's kingdom.”

Axiom: Severe emotional loss functions as an evolutionary catalyst toward the Divine.

Setting & Personae: Mukunda (age 11), brother Ananta, Father. Calcutta and Bareilly.

Forensic Content: Mukunda experiences a terrifying clairvoyant premonition of his mother's death while traveling. She passes before he can reach Calcutta. In his agony, he prays ceaselessly to the Divine Mother. Eldest brother Ananta later delivers an amulet left by their mother: a miraculous gold talisman bestowed by a sadhu, prophesied to dissolve into the ether the moment Mukunda meets his destined guru.

Chapter 3: The Saint with Two Bodies (Swami Pranabananda)

Axiom: Bilocation operates via conscious lifetronic projection across space.

Forensic Content: Pranabananda physically materializes before Mukunda in Calcutta while simultaneously manifesting before Mukunda's father miles away in Burdwan to deliver a written railway message.
