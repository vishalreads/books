The Light Side Four-Pillar Grammar

1. Planets (Actors): Who is performing the internal psychological drive.

2. Signs (Costumes): How the actor expresses their personality and emotional style.

3. Houses (Stages): Where in physical or social life the drama takes place.

4. Aspects (Scripts): How the actors communicate, collaborate, or clash.

Elemental Dynamics

Fire: Dynamic inspiration, action, and forward momentum.

Earth: Physical grounding, tangibility, logistics, and resource management.

Air: Intellectual oxygen, communication, conceptual links, and social logic.

Water: Emotional depth, psychic empathy, soul connections, and gut feeling.
