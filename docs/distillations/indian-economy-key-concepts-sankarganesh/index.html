National Income Revision (2015)

GDP_MP = GVA_Basic + (Product Taxes - Product Subsidies)

Shifted from Factor Cost to Market Prices as headline growth. Aligns with UN SNA 2008 and integrates MCA-21 database.

Money Multiplier Mechanics

m = (1 + c) / (c + r)

Determined by Currency-Deposit ratio (c) and Reserve-Deposit ratio (r via CRR). High-powered money M0 multiplies into broad money M3.

Deficit Accounting Geometry

Fiscal Deficit = Total Exp - (Revenue Receipts + Non-Debt Capital Receipts)

Represents total government borrowing. Primary Deficit isolates current fiscal stance by subtracting net interest payments.

Bond Yield Invariant Law

Price ↑ ──► Yield ↓ | Price ↓ ──► Yield ↑

Inverted yield curves indicate short-term rates exceeding long-term rates, serving as a reliable leading indicator of recession.

Real Effective Exchange Rate

REER = NEER × (Domestic Prices / Foreign Prices)

Measures trade competitiveness against a 40-currency basket. REER > 100 indicates domestic currency overvaluation.

Fisher's Equation of Exchange

M × V = P × T

Direct mathematical link between money supply expansion and general price level inflation when velocity and real transactions are stable.

Article 280: Finance Commission

Vertical Devolution: 41% net share of Union divisible taxes to States (15th FC).

Horizontal Criteria: Income distance (45%), Population (15%), Area (15%), Demographic performance (12.5%), Forest & ecology (10%), Tax effort (2.5%).

Article 279A: GST Council

Voting Architecture: Center = 1/3 weight; All States = 2/3 weight. Decision threshold = 75% majority.

Guarantees cooperative federalism where neither Center nor States can unilaterally dictate indirect tax policy.

Inflation Targeting Framework

Target: 4% CPI-C with ±2% band (Section 45ZA RBI Act 1934).

Monetary Policy Committee: 6 members (3 RBI, 3 Central Govt). Anchor rate: Repo; corridor defined by MSF (ceiling) and SDF (floor).

WTO Subsidies Framework (AoA)

Green Box: Non-distorting subsidies (R&D, decoupled income) allowed without limits.

Amber Box: Trade-distorting support (MSP, inputs) capped at 10% for developing nations; protected by the 2013 Bali Peace Clause.
