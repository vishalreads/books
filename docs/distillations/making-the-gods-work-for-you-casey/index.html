Visionary Activist Astrology: Principles & Astrological Grammar

Caroline Casey defines astrology not as passive fortune-telling, but as an ancient, poetic language of participatory engagement with living cosmic archetypes. Serving the Archetypes: "Working for the God, or Being Worked by the God"

Casey revives the animistic polytheism of classical Hermeticism. Planetary forces are living daemons or deities residing within the psyche and the cosmos. If an individual does not consciously apprentice to a planetary god through respectful ritual, creative craft, and ethical service, the neglected god erupts pathologically as compulsion, neurosis, addiction, or catastrophic external drama.
